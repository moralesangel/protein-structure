How it works

1 · GraphEach residue is a node at its Cα; edges join residues within 8 Å.
2 · FeaturesResidue identity one-hot, hydrophobicity, charge, and relative SASA.
3 · Message passingThree distance-aware layers mix each residue with its spatial neighbours.
4 · ScoreA head outputs one binding probability per residue.

Training used focal loss because only about 7% of residues contact a ligand, and the checkpoint was selected on validation PR-AUC rather than accuracy — with a base rate that low, a model predicting "never" would still score 93% accurate.
